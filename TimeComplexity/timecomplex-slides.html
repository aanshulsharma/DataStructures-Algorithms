⏱️ Time Complexity

Explained with a Deck of Cards 🃏

A visual, story-driven guide to Big O notation

🤔 The Big Question

"If the number of items I'm searching through keeps growing,
how much MORE time will it take me?"

That question is exactly what Time Complexity answers.

It's just a way to describe: "as things get bigger, how much slower do I get?"

O(N) · LINEAR

Story 1: Shuffled Deck

Cards thrown in random order. Find the 6 of Spades.

🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴

Only way: check every card, one by one, until found.

The Pattern

  • 10 cards → check up to 10
  • 100 cards → check up to 100
  • 1,000 cards → check up to 1,000

Double the cards → double the work.

📌 This is O(N) — work grows at the same rate as the input.

O(log N) · LOGARITHMIC

Story 2: Sorted Deck

Same cards, now sorted. Find the 7 of Hearts.

Step 1 — open the middle card

🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴 6 🎴 🎴 🎴 🎴

7 > 6 → must be on the right side.

Left half? completely ignored 🚮

Step 2 — open the middle of what's left

🎴 🎴 🎴 7 🎴

✅ Found it — in just 3 checks, out of 10 cards!

The Pattern

Every step throws away half of what remains.

📌 This is O(log N) — work barely grows, even with huge input.

O(1) · CONSTANT

Story 3: Numbered Bowls

6 bowls, each holding blackberries matching its position.

🍇1️⃣   🍇🍇2️⃣   🍇🍇🍇3️⃣   🍇🍇🍇🍇4️⃣   🍇🍇🍇🍇🍇5️⃣   🍇🍇🍇🍇🍇🍇6️⃣

"How many berries in bowl 5?" 🤔

No searching needed — go straight to bowl 5. Answer: 5. ✅

📌 This is O(1) — work never changes, no matter the input size.

O(N²) · QUADRATIC

Story 4: Two Decks

Pick a card from Deck 1 (say 8) → search all of Deck 2 for its match.

8 → 🎴🎴🎴🎴🎴8🎴🎴

Now repeat for EVERY card in Deck 1

for each card in Deck 1 (N cards):
    search all of Deck 2 (N cards)

A loop inside a loop → N work, done N times.

CardsChecks (worst case)
10100
1,0001,000,000

📌 This is O(N²) — grows by the square of N.

O(N³) · CUBIC

Story 5: Three Decks

Same idea, but 3 nested loops — one per deck.

for each card in Deck 1:
  for each card in Deck 2:
    search all of Deck 3

📌 O(N³) — every extra deck multiplies the work by N again.

O(2ⁿ) · EXPONENTIAL

Story 6: Flipping Coins

1 coin → H/T

2 coins → possibilities double

HH   HT   TH   TT  (4 outcomes)

3 coins → double again → 8 outcomes

📌 O(2ⁿ) — every new input multiplies the work by 2.

⚠️ Grows so fast it's often unmanageable for large inputs.

O(N!) · FACTORIAL

Story 7: Seating Passengers

3 seats, 3 passengers. How many seating orders?

Seat 1: 3 choices   →   Seat 2: 2 choices   →   Seat 3: 1 choice

3 × 2 × 1 = 6 arrangements

📌 O(N!) — grows even faster than O(2ⁿ). Quickly impossible to compute.

❓ Why "Big O"?

We describe speed as the growth rate relative to input size.

Input 10 → 10 sec. Input 1,000 → 1,000 sec. That's linear — "order of N".

O = Order Of — the upper bound of how fast the work grows.

🎉 That's Big O!

Same kind of problem, wildly different amounts of work.
